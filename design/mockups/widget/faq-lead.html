Lead capture

SCREEN-INVENTORY §2.8 · W43

A request the bot can't serve directly becomes a stored lead (leads table: name · phone · source · message · status · created_at — the PII stays in the store, never in this page). If the write fails, the customer gets the infra-class W50 instead — never a false "we've got it".

W43Lead captured
I'm planning a stag-do for six — can someone get back to me with options?12:35
✓ Thanks! We've got your details and someone from the team will reach out soon.12:35

The confirm treatment (oxide edge + check) marks a committed write — same visual promise as a booking receipt: “we've got it” is only said when the row exists.
